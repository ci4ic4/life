Squirrels & Pine Martens — Ecology on a Torus

A spatial contact process on a torus: two rival prey species compete for empty ground. Red squirrels (native) and grey squirrels (invasive) each colonise an adjacent empty cell at their own per-species birth rate β; an occupied cell survives each tick with probability σ.

Why grey tends to win

Grey's default β is set higher than red's — the invasive-species advantage. With no predation yet, a higher β simply out-colonises empty ground faster, so grey expands and red's share shrinks over time. Watch the two lines in the population chart cross.

Controls

  • β red / β grey / σ — the ecology knobs; raise σ for longer-lived populations, β for faster colonisation.
