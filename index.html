Life

Browser simulations, each a single self-contained HTML file. No build step, no bundler, no server — open one and it runs. Most are Conway's Life variants rendered on a 3D torus; the newest is not a cellular automaton at all.

Stellar physics

☀

Stellar Evolution

Four stars from 0.5 to 25 solar masses, evolving on one clock — Lane-Emden structure, proton-proton and CNO burning, adaptive timesteps. Not a cellular automaton: interiors cross-sectioned on a log radius axis, tracked on an HR diagram.

physics core

Reading them. Every simulation has a Help button explaining the rules and what is worth watching. The ones marked GPU fall back to CPU automatically on smaller grids; each checks its GPU path against the CPU reference at startup.

Reproducibility. The seeded simulations replay exactly from a seed plus settings, and can copy that setup to the clipboard for sharing.

How one was built. The Enemy of My Enemy follows the squirrel simulation through its eight stages — including the two tuned, tested, confidently wrong ideas that had to be torn out, and the finding that matters most: a predator with no preference still crashes the greys, and the reds die anyway.

Under them. The rules live in plain modules — life-stats.js, life-ecology-core.js, life-conveyor-core.js — with no DOM, shared between the pages and Node's test runner. node --test runs the suite.
