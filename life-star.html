Stellar Evolution — Cross-Section

Four stars — 0.5, 1, 8 and 25 solar masses — modelled shell by shell: Lane-Emden structure, proton-proton and CNO burning, adaptive timesteps. All four run on the same real physical clock, not a normalised one, which is why the massive star finishes its life in millions of years while the smallest is barely underway. Pick a star below to see its cross-section; the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram further down tracks all four at once.

Hertzsprung-Russell diagram

Effective temperature against luminosity, drawn the conventional way: temperature increases to the left, both axes logarithmic. The four dots landing on a single diagonal band as they evolve is the model's own main sequence — nothing is drawn to suggest it is there beforehand. (This model's Sun runs hotter than the real one for reasons noted in the project's design docs; the four tracks are self-consistent with each other, which is what the diagram is checking.)
