Stellar Evolution — Cross-Section

A single star's interior, modelled shell by shell: Lane-Emden structure, proton-proton and CNO burning, adaptive timesteps. Not a cellular automaton — one star, evolving under real (if simplified) stellar physics. Radius is drawn on a log scale because a star's radius changes by orders of magnitude over its life.
